# Worst Gig Ever — friend beta

Thanks for testing. This takes about five minutes to install and about ten
minutes to play through once.

**This is a private test build. Please do not redistribute it publicly.**

---

## What you need

- An Android phone or tablet running **Android 7.0 or newer**.
- About **190 MB** free, and a **Wi-Fi connection** to download it — the file is
  large because the music is uncompressed in this test build.
- The game is **landscape only**, so turn your phone sideways.

There is no iPhone build yet.

---

## Installing

1. **Download the APK** from the link the owner sent you. Open it on the phone
   itself rather than on a computer.

2. **Open the downloaded file.** Tap it in your notification shade, or find it
   in Files under *Downloads* and tap it there.

3. **Allow the install if Android asks.** Because the file did not come from the
   Play Store, Android asks once whether the app you downloaded it with — your
   browser, or Files — is allowed to install apps. Say yes for that app. This is
   Android's normal flow for installing something outside the store.

4. **You may see a Play Protect warning.** Something like *"unsafe app blocked"*
   or *"app not recognised"*. This is expected: the build is signed with a test
   certificate rather than a Play Store one, and Play Protect flags anything it
   has not seen before. Choose the option to install anyway.

5. **Tap Install**, and wait — it is a big file.

6. **Open Worst Gig Ever.**

7. **Choose your language.** English or Português (BR). This is asked once, on
   the first launch only. You can change it later from the button on the title
   screen.

8. **Play.** The opening story runs for about half a minute and can be skipped
   with the button in the corner.

If you would rather not get past the Play Protect warning, that is completely
fine — tell the owner and skip it. Do not disable any other security setting to
install this.

---

## Uninstalling

Long-press the icon and choose *Uninstall*, or find it in
*Settings → Apps → Worst Gig Ever*. Nothing is left behind and nothing is sent
anywhere.

Note for later: if this game ever reaches the Play Store, you will probably have
to uninstall this test version before installing that one, because Android will
not replace a test-signed app with a store-signed one. Your scores and your
setlist would go with it.

---

## What the game does not do

So you know what you are installing:

- no ads;
- no purchases, and no payment of any kind;
- no account, no login;
- no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting;
- nothing is uploaded, and no data leaves your phone.

Everything the game remembers — your scores, your progress, your setlist, your
language — is a single file in the app's own storage, and it is deleted when you
uninstall.
