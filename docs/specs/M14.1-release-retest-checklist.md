# M14.1 release retest — checklist

**One question.** Does the game feel responsive to the touch on a release
build? Everything else about M14.1 was answered long ago: the owner approved
the visuals on the M14 APK, and the rendering corrections were measured in a
controlled browser comparison at a 36.3% reduction in JavaScript time. What
has never happened is somebody tapping a *release* build on the phone.

**Why a release build specifically.** Every device session so far has run the
development client: a **debug** build on **JSC**, with dev-mode assertions, no
minification, and JavaScript delivered over a cable from Metro. Open item 5
says not to judge frame pacing on it, and that is not a formality — the
overheads it adds are exactly the ones the question is about. A debug build
that feels sluggish proves nothing, and one that feels fine proves less.

## Before you start

Turn the click **off** for the first round. It is new, and the point of this
session is not to find out whether you like it — you already said you do. It
is to isolate the feel of the touch.

## The four things to feel for

1. **First hit of the round.** Tap the very first bottle. Does the break land
   with the tap, or a beat after it? The M13 report was that the first hit felt
   late.
2. **Repeated hits under pressure.** Play into the last third of Stage 3, where
   an object arrives every 850 ms. Does the tenth break feel like the first?
3. **The Groove Pad.** Tap the pad on the beat, deliberately, twenty times.
   PERFECT should be reachable. If you can *hear* your tap land right and still
   read GOOD, that is latency, not timing.
4. **A whole round, uninterrupted.** Stage 3 start to finish. Any stutter,
   hitch, or moment where the stage animation and the pad disagree.

## What not to re-litigate

- **Difficulty.** Frozen at M13.1 and validated. If the show feels hard, that
  is the answer it is supposed to give.
- **The art.** Approved at M14 on device.
- **The Encore.** Stage 4 is M17 and is deliberately harder. It is not evidence
  about performance; its objects are meant to be hard to reach.
- **The show's music drifting against the beat.** Known, and the last open
  piece of M16. Do not report it as a bug.

## Recording the outcome

One of three, in your own words, plus which stage you played:

- `PERFORMANCE_OK` — the touch feels immediate; M14.1 closes.
- `PERFORMANCE_STILL_SLOW` — it still lags. Say *where*: first hit, under
  pressure, on the pad, or everywhere.
- `PERFORMANCE_MIXED` — fine in one place and not another. That is the most
  useful answer of the three, because it points at a cause.

If the verdict is not `PERFORMANCE_OK`, the next lever is **Hermes**.
`jsEngine` is still `jsc` in `app.json`, and switching it is a one-line change
with a native rebuild — but it is a change to the runtime under a round whose
difficulty is frozen, so it wants its own build and its own comparison rather
than being bundled into a milestone.
