# M15 Story Still Provenance

- Recorded: 2026-09-03.
- Scope: the five opening-story illustrations shipped in `assets/art/story/`.
- Milestone: `docs/specs/M15-story-briefings-and-two-stages.md`.

These are **flattened narrative stills**. Nothing is composited against them,
nothing moves inside them, and no hitbox is measured from them, so AGENTS.md
rule 16 has nothing to isolate here. They are separate from the 33-file Pack 1
gameplay contract and are recorded in the `story` section of
`assets/manifest/asset-manifest.json` rather than in `assets`.

## Origin

All five were generated by the owner outside this repository with the OpenAI
built-in image generation tool, from the project's own approved characters and
environment art. No third-party artwork, commercial band reference, real-person
likeness, or branded alcohol label was supplied to any of them (AGENTS.md rules
12 and 15).

The generation prompts, original execution filenames, and per-image notes are
preserved unmodified alongside the source PNGs, one `README.md` per directory
under `design-reference/intro-*/`. The character identities they build on are
recorded in `docs/assets/M14-V2-PRODUCTION-PROVENANCE.md`.

was retouched, recomposed, or substituted (AGENTS.md rule 14).

Two sources have a V2. Both V2s are the ones shipped, and both were chosen for
recorded reasons rather than preference:

- **Arrival.** V1 shows three members; V2 adds the drummer, so the arriving
  group is the same four people the poster and the performance still show.
- **Sound desk.** The owner rejected V1 as looking deliberate. V2 replaces the
  pouring grip with a knocked-over mug and a failed catch, which is the story
  the game needs: the crowd turns because of an accident, not sabotage.

The V1 files remain in `design-reference/` and are not shipped.

## Conditioning

Source: 1672x941 PNG, roughly 2.3 MB each, 11.5 MB in total.
Shipped: 1600x900 JPEG at quality 85, roughly 470 KB each, 2.3 MB in total.

```
sips -Z 1600 <source>.png --out <target>.jpg -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 85
```

JPEG is the one deviation from the project's PNG art pipeline, and it is
confined to this directory. These images are opaque, full-bleed and
photographic in density, with no alpha to preserve — 9.2 MB of the bundle for
five pictures shown once would have been paid for nothing. Gameplay art is
unaffected and `npm run validate:art` still enforces the PNG contract over all
33 required Pack 1 files.

1600x900 is a slight downscale of the 1672-wide source rather than an upscale,
and is 16:9, matching the reference canvas the rest of the game is authored to.

## Verification

`tests/storyIntro.test.ts` asserts, for all five files: JPEG signature, exact
1600x900 dimensions, 16:9 within tolerance, an 800 KB per-file budget, presence
on disk, a manifest entry, and that no story still has leaked into the Pack 1
`assets` contract.
