# Generate Bassist Character Pack

Read `00-style-bible.md` first.

Create one original fictional bassist reference and preserve it across:
- `assets/art/band/bassist_idle.png`
- `assets/art/band/bassist_loop_a.png`
- `assets/art/band/bassist_loop_b.png`
- `assets/art/band/bassist_hit_reaction.png`
- `assets/art/band/bassist_dodge.png`

640×900 transparent PNG target canvas.

Drummer-POV orientation: the bassist stands between the drummer and audience,
facing the audience in a readable back or three-quarter-back pose. Do not pose
the character as if the camera were in the crowd looking toward the stage.

Ambient loop: small knee/torso bob, bass neck angle shift, head nod, stable feet anchor. Hit reaction: comic flinch/jolt. Dodge: clear lean/duck away from projectile while instrument remains with character.

No real-musician likeness, logo, or branded instrument marks.
