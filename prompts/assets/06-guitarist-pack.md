# Generate Guitarist Character Pack

Read `00-style-bible.md` first.

Create one original fictional guitarist reference and preserve it across:
- `assets/art/band/guitarist_idle.png`
- `assets/art/band/guitarist_loop_a.png`
- `assets/art/band/guitarist_loop_b.png`
- `assets/art/band/guitarist_hit_reaction.png`
- `assets/art/band/guitarist_dodge.png`

640×900 transparent PNG target canvas.

Drummer-POV orientation: the guitarist stands between the drummer and
audience, facing the audience in a readable back or three-quarter-back pose.
Do not pose the character as if the camera were in the crowd looking toward
the stage.

Ambient loop: subtle head/body bounce, guitar angle/strumming arm changes, stable ground anchor, intentionally low-frame. Hit reaction: comic surprised jolt. Dodge: readable lean/duck with same character/guitar.

No real-musician likeness, logos, branded instrument marks.
