# Generate Crowd Pack — 3 Frame Motion

Read `00-style-bible.md` first.

Create:
- `assets/art/crowd/crowd_back.png` — 1920×520 transparent PNG
- `assets/art/crowd/crowd_front_01.png` — 1920×420 transparent PNG
- `assets/art/crowd/crowd_front_02.png` — 1920×420 transparent PNG
- `assets/art/crowd/crowd_front_03.png` — 1920×420 transparent PNG

Front frames depict the **same crowd composition** with small pose changes: arms move, heads/bodies bob, hands/fists/horns change height. No person teleports or changes identity.

Drummer-POV orientation is mandatory: the crowd is beyond the band and faces
the stage/drummer/camera. Show faces and front torsos, not a row of backs.
Raised hands extend toward the stage. The rear crowd follows the same
orientation.

Emotional tone: the audience is comically furious at the terrible band—big
exaggerated scowls, theatrical booing, silly outrage, shaking fists, and
over-the-top frustrated expressions. Keep it playful and absurd rather than
threatening, grim, or violent. No gore or injuries.

Gameplay fantasy: several crowd members must clearly read as the source of the
incoming throws. Across the three frames, include exaggerated wind-up,
release, and follow-through poses aimed toward the drummer/camera. Keep actual
beer bottles and mugs out of the crowd bitmap—the runtime projectile assets
remain isolated and fly on their own authored arcs.

The 3 frames loop in intentionally choppy retro rhythm. Keep central projectile corridor readable. No real people/logos/readable branded shirts.
