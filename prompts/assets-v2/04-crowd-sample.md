# V2 Crowd Sample Proof

Read `00-style-bible.md` first.

Use case: `stylized-concept`
Asset type: mobile game crowd-layer visual-direction proof
Input images: none. Keeping the band image out of the generation input is a
deliberate identity firewall: match the written style and palette contract
below without copying any performer face, hair, clothing, body, or instrument.
Primary request: redraw one front-crowd sample in the Stylized Punk Arcade
Cartoon direction, substantially simpler than Pack 1 while retaining readable
fictional faces and playful hostility.

**Detail budget, and the reason this prompt exists.** The first attempt came
back with heavier black outlines, more per-face rendering than the band, and a
muted brown/olive/gray wardrobe. That is the failure to avoid. The crowd is
supporting scenery: it must be *visibly simpler* than the performer proof set —
thinner contour weight, fewer internal lines per figure, and no
face rendered at performer detail except two or three in the front row. If a
crowd figure would survive being cropped out and used as a band member, it is
too detailed.
Subject: a row of varied fictional punk/garage-rock audience members facing
the drummer/camera, theatrically booing, scowling, raising fists or horns, and
showing empty-handed throwing wind-up/follow-through poses; energetic and
absurd, not threatening.
Composition/framing: wide crowd strip on one flat warm-gray studio card;
grouped left and right
clusters; maintain a calmer low-detail central projectile channel; upper bodies
and selected full figures visible; no bottle or mug baked into the crowd.
Style/medium: bold simplified mobile-arcade shape language; chunky confident
contours; clean 2D crowd illustration; strong grouped silhouettes; large
clothing and hair shapes; selected front faces; one shadow tone.
Color palette: grouped charcoal masses with controlled magenta, teal, amber,
green, blue, and orange accents.
Constraints: crowd faces the camera/stage; readable hands and expressions;
flat warm-gray background; no text, logos, real people, or watermark.
Avoid: tattoos, fishnet grids, tiny seams, individual hair strands, equal
detail on every face, featureless silhouette wall, violent imagery.

Do not reuse any band identity token: no teal shaved-side crest, mustard vest,
magenta repair, white high-tops, dark high puff with teal vest, orange shirt,
purple cargo trousers, amber boots, flame-orange hair, electric-blue cropped
jacket, magenta plaid trousers, teal boots, red bass, cream guitar, or
microphone.
