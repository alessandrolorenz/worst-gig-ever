# V2 Performer Lineup Gate

Read `00-style-bible.md` first.

Use case: `stylized-concept`
Asset type: mobile-game character-family visual-direction proof
Primary request: create one original three-character lineup for Worst Gig Ever
in the Stylized Punk Arcade Cartoon direction. Preserve the locked identity
tokens below, but do not trace, imitate, or reproduce Pack 1's detailed comic
rendering. The three people must have unmistakably different face geometry,
body shape, height, posture, and energy.
Scene/backdrop: plain warm-gray studio card with no scenery and no cast shadows.
Composition/framing: wide horizontal lineup, three separated full-body front
three-quarter views on one baseline, no overlap and no labels. From left to
right: vocalist, bassist, guitarist.
Style/medium: bold mobile arcade cartoon; chunky confident contour; simplified
anatomy; large flat color masses; exactly one cel-shadow tone; very few internal
lines; expressive adult faces; readable at thumbnail size. Not anime, not
realistic comic-book rendering, not corporate vector art.

Vocalist: medium height, compact wiry body, angular triangular face and sharp
eyebrows, asymmetrical forward stance, messy teal crest with one shaved side,
mustard sleeveless vest as one large shape, magenta shirt, black narrow jeans
with one magenta repair, white high-top boots, generic wired microphone.
Defiant and impatient. Do not give the vocalist the guitarist's long face or
height.

Bassist: shortest and widest of the three, broad shoulders and hips, thick
arms and legs, square-round face with broad nose and heavy brows, low stable
stance, brown skin, dark curly high puff with shaved sides, teal vest,
burnt-orange shirt, purple cargo trousers simplified into large pockets,
amber boots, red-and-cream generic bass. Grounded and stubborn. Do not slim the
bassist or reuse either wiry silhouette.

Guitarist: clearly tallest and narrowest, long limbs, long narrow face with a
prominent nose and half-lidded theatrical expression, crooked S-curve posture,
light-brown skin, one large flame-orange hair mass, electric-blue cropped
jacket, dark shirt, broad magenta-and-black trouser checks, teal boots, cream
offset generic guitar with teal pickguard. Twitchy and comically self-serious.
Do not reuse the vocalist's face, stance, or hair construction.

Constraints: three distinct original fictional adults; faces clearly visible;
hands correctly hold microphone/instruments; no duplicate facial template;
no duplicate body proportions; no real-person likeness; no logos, brands,
text, watermark, gore, tattoos, chains, fishnets, tiny seams, dense folds,
individual hair strands, cross-hatching, or microtexture.
