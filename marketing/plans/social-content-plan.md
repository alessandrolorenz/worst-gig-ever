# Four-week lightweight social campaign

Eight feed posts total, cross-posted to Instagram and Facebook rather than doubled. One or two optional Story/Reel ideas per week, seven total. Batch captions and exports once per week; skip the optional second story whenever it stops being fun. Dates remain relative to owner approval and access to a shareable test build.

Five pillars are covered: gameplay chaos (P01/P02/S01/S02), humor/world (P03/P07/S03/S07), challenge (P04/P08/S06), music/setlist (P05/P06/S05), behind the game (S04).

Reuse: vertical art can support TikTok once an actual video is assembled; Threads/X can use square art and one short hook; Reddit should use an honest developer post, disclose context and follow each community's rules; store pages need fresh captures and a separate specification check. No accounts, scheduling, publishing or poll integration are performed here.

Measure only a few real signals after posting: replies asking to play, test-build requests, and comments that correctly describe the game. No invented engagement targets or promised reach. English and pt-BR captions live in `../social/copy/`; choose one language per post.
