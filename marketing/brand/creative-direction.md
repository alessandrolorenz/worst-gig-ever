# Creative direction

The drummer is trying to finish a small-bar gig while bottles fly. The band plays on. Somehow they get paid. That joke must read in five seconds.

Use the existing angular 2D ink contours, simplified cel shading, expressive faces, battered red drum shells with worn gold stars, charcoal/purple rooms and amber, magenta, teal and blue lights. Keep grunge on surfaces, not over important silhouettes. One clear action per image; bottles and sticks must read at thumbnail size.

Canonical cast: slim tan-skinned black-haired drummer in a black T-shirt and dark jeans; teal-mohawk vocalist in mustard vest, magenta top, black trousers and white boots; stocky dark-skinned bassist with puff hair, orange shirt, teal vest, purple cargo trousers, ochre boots and red bass; tall slim orange-haired guitarist in blue jacket, magenta plaid flares, teal boots and cream guitar with teal pickguard. Payout owner: broad middle-aged man with swept-back dark hair, heavy brows, charcoal suit, burgundy vest and cigar.

New poses, same people. No polished 3D, anime, photorealism, heroic concert spectacle, new costumes, celebrity likenesses, branded drinks or generic rock-band substitutions. Beer is an inconvenient comic prop, not a glamorous product.

All art is text-free. Titles, hooks and localized captions remain separate. Maintain central action clearance for format recompositions, but do not force every image into the same layout. Stories reserve calm top and bottom areas for optional copy and platform overlays. Final channel exports use proportional scaling only; no stretching or destructive cropping of key subjects.

Selection rule: generate the six concepts, inspect silhouette/action/identity, then adapt the strongest compositions to landscape, square and vertical. Preserve alternatives for owner review. The owner alone assigns KEEP / REVISE / REJECT.
