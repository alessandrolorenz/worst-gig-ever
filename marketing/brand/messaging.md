# Messaging

Product name, untranslated: **Worst Gig Ever**.

Primary: “Can you survive the impossible gig?”
Secondary: “Survive the show. Keep the groove.”
Custom Setlist: “You survived ours. Now make it worse.”
Short sequence: “Keep the beat. Dodge the bottles. Drink the beer. Survive the gig.”

pt-BR adaptations: “Você sobrevive ao show impossível?” / “Sobreviva ao show. Segure o groove.” / “Você sobreviveu ao nosso. Agora faça pior.”

Talk like a small indie project with a sense of humor. Short sentences, one joke, one invitation. No fake urgency, reviews, player counts, awards or unsupported superlatives. Use at most 2–4 relevant hashtags from the proposed pool in the copy files.

Verified claim sources: `game/audio/musicCatalogue.ts` contains eleven production library entries; `game/audio/setlist.ts` requires four distinct choices; `game/state/appFlow.ts` unlocks Custom Setlist after clearing the final stage; `game/levels/stages.ts` defines four stages. Groove and Defense are distinct results measures; beer is a statistic and the payout is narrative (`game/state/gigPayout.ts`). Do not imply real-money earnings.

Do not promise multiplayer, rankings, accounts, cloud saves, achievements, endless stages or hundreds of tracks. Do not imply Custom Setlist is available before completing the show. Do not invent store availability: pre-release CTA is “Want to try the friend test?”; “Get the game” is reserved for a verified live destination.
